## Reference observations and limits

The supplied ONYX recording is 1245.44 seconds long, 1920 × 1152, approximately
29.78 encoded frames/second. Timestamped samples span the recording; detailed
sequences cover 14.8–16.3 s and 88–91.5 s. The 15–16 s sequence shows feed
collecting close to the green source edge rather than making a long stream.
Around 16 s the roughly 4365-mass source has an approximately 86-pixel radius;
the visible feed radii are about 4–6 pixels. The selected 9.5-unit radius gives
about 5 pixels at the corresponding scale. The in-game fragment counter shows
a 32-cell limit; the user's later request overrides it with a 16-cell limit.

These are visual observations and explicit tuning choices, not recovered
server constants or a guarantee of exact parity. Camera zoom, interpolation,
and other players obscure some trajectories. Existing calibrated movement,
split impulse, launch damping and merge timings are retained; this revision
changes FFA feed, the automatic split threshold and fragment cap. Mega and
Dual are compared against a saved fixture of their original full profiles.

## Crystal charging and Vintage Atlas

FFA still starts with 32 viruses, while the shared reproduction ceiling is 64.
Feeding no longer moves an unrelated virus when the initial population is full.
Charge accumulates from actual pellet mass: 175 mass releases a new crystal in
the incoming feed direction (seven FFA pellets). Outward shots reflect inward at
the arena wall. At the ceiling, crystals retain charge until a slot opens; eating
a charged crystal also awards its stored mass. These rules run identically in
the authoritative server and local game. Mega/Dual movement profiles stay unchanged.

Theme > Border now controls the independent edge color, 1–24 screen-pixel width,
Solid / Prism flow style, glow switch, glow color, spread and intensity. A single
closed stroke keeps Prism corners seamless. Width and glow stay readable at
wide zoom and respect the same preferences in spectator mode.

Theme > Sectors adds the Vintage Atlas 5×5 grid, independent line and label
colors, screen-pixel line width, coordinate size and corner/center placement.
Letters advance across columns, numbers down rows, matching the existing
visibility sectors. New and upgraded clients start with sectors visible; later
changes to the visibility switch are preserved. Background color also applies
when artwork is disabled. Border and sector sections preview the actual guide
renderer. Import/export, local persistence, search, discard and reset include
all new controls.

`python scripts/check-atlas-browser.py` changes these controls through the UI,

## Tag voice

Tag-only WebRTC voice is integrated into the existing live server, with its
panel below the team/connection rows. It has microphone mute, listen-only mode,
per-player local mute and volume, master listening volume, deafen and speaking
indicators. Membership is isolated by the server's stored tag and game room;
multibox creates one voice endpoint. Leaving/changing a session or disconnecting
stops microphone tracks. Voice controls remain visible when voice is active in
the lobby. No voice starts recording on page load.

See [VOICE-SETUP.md](VOICE-SETUP.md) for HTTPS/WSS, STUN/TURN configuration, the
12-person mesh limit, protocol behavior and validation scope. Server signaling
payloads are bounded to 16 KiB, audio-only SDP is limited to 12 KiB, and per-socket
voice signaling is throttled. Voice audio is not carried in game snapshots.

`python scripts/check-voice-browser.py` verifies real, decoded two-way WebRTC
audio using a synthetic microphone, actual local gain/mute behavior and capture
cleanup. `voice-browser-check.json` and desktop/mobile panel screenshots are
included in the evidence directory. Internet TURN connectivity still requires
deployment configuration and a test on the deployed hosts.

## Virus, feed and multibox fixes

- Viruses have stable identities. Their flight and charge radius interpolate
  between network snapshots; consumed viruses disappear immediately. Resetting
  the renderer clears their animation state. Cell coordinates retain hundredth
  precision on the wire instead of jumping between whole world units.
- A virus must be swallowed before a burst. Daughter fragments start at the
  struck cell's center and move out through the shared movement solver, with
  origin metadata identifying their source. The fragment cap and total mass
  remain intact. Consuming a virus at the cap no longer resets merge timers.
- Each feed pellet receives a stored ordinary food color on emission. Tab and
  active/inactive multibox colors never recolor it. The existing single food
  color setting still applies when rainbow food is disabled.
- Ownership is validated before the second socket's initial spawn. Both initial
  spawn and respawn use nearby sibling fragments, choose the nearest fragment
  to the requested point, and search locally when blocked. A fully crowded
  neighborhood can delay spawning instead of teleporting the connection away.
- Respawn protection still blocks other players, including people in the same
  tag. Your own other connection can eat the fresh piece immediately when it
  satisfies the normal mass ratio and overlap rules. Equal masses cannot eat
  one another. Ownership keys remain absent from public snapshots.

Validation: **171 Node tests pass**. `python scripts/check-multi-virus-browser.py`
reproduces initial pairing, Tab respawn, immediate owned eating, fixed feed
colors, 16-piece bursts and continuous virus flight in the actual local and
WebSocket clients. It uses fixtures inside its own test process, with no
